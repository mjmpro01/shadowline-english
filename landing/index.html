How it works

One line at a time. Every day a little closer.

  1. 1

    Listen

    Pick a scene from the library and hear one line from a native speaker — as many times as you like.

  2. 2

    Shadow

    Record yourself saying the same line, copying its melody, stress and timing as closely as you can.

  3. 3

    Compare & improve

    Get a score and see your pitch and rhythm drawn over the original. Try again and watch the lines meet.

How do I say the “th” in “through”?

Put the tip of your tongue lightly between your teeth and blow air gently — no voice. Then glide straight into “roo”. Try it slowly: th…roo, then speed up.

Why shadowing

Your ear leads. Your voice follows.

Shadowing — repeating native speech right after you hear it — is how interpreters and actors train. It builds the rhythm, stress and intonation that make English sound natural, which grammar drills never touch.

Shadowline makes the method measurable. Instead of guessing whether you sounded right, you see it — and short daily sessions add up fast.

Safe, private and encouraging

Built for learners of every age. Signing in with Google shares only your name and email address. Your recordings are never shown to anyone else, there are no ads, and you can download or delete all of your data at any time.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Shadowline English free?

Yes. You can sign up and practise for free. If we ever add paid extras, we will say so clearly first.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Shadowline runs in a modern browser on your phone, tablet or computer. You only need a microphone — and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

What level of English do I need?

Any level. Beginners can start with short, slow lines; advanced learners can polish fast, natural speech. You choose the clips.

How is my pronunciation scored?

We compare the pitch, loudness and timing of your recording with the original speaker, line by line, and show you where they match and where they drift apart.

Who can hear my recordings?

Only you. Recordings are scored on our own servers, never shown to other learners, and you can delete them whenever you like.

Is it suitable for children?

Shadowline is designed as a safe, encouraging space for young learners. Children under 13 should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission.
