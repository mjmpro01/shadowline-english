Shadowline English

Shadowline English helps you practise speaking English by shadowing real video clips. Listen to a line spoken by a native speaker, record yourself saying the same line, and get a score with feedback on your pronunciation, rhythm and intonation. Save new words as flashcards, ask an AI tutor for help, and track your progress over time.

Signing in with Google shares only your name and email address. Your recordings are never shown to anyone else, and you can download or delete your data at any time.
